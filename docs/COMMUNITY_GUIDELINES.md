# Lemegeton Community Guidelines

**Effective date: November 3, 2026**

These guidelines apply to community features and submissions made through Lemegeton.

## Respect other members

Do not use submissions, discussions, reports, or other bot features to harass, threaten, impersonate, dox, or target people. Do not spam submissions, manipulate votes, abuse reports, or attempt to disrupt a server.

## Give credit and respect rights

Credit the original creator and link to a legitimate source where possible. Submit only content you have permission to share. Do not upload or use community archives to distribute pirated copies, full chapters, books, films, episodes, artwork collections, or music files. Archive entries are intended for attribution, metadata, short excerpts where lawful, and links—not rehosting media.

Quotes are limited by the bot to 280 characters. Do not submit song lyrics or long copyrighted passages. A source link does not prove ownership or permission.

## Mark spoilers

Use the stated chapter, volume, episode, season, or other spoiler cutoff in discussions. Keep replies within that boundary and mark later material using Discord's spoiler formatting. Give submissions accurate spoiler labels and avoid misleading other readers.

## Keep submissions appropriate

Use age-restricted features only in channels Discord marks as NSFW and follow the rules of the server where you post. Do not submit illegal, exploitative, threatening, or otherwise prohibited material. External links must use HTTPS where the feature requires it and must not be used to mislead members.

## Moderation

Moderators may reject, hide, or remove material and restrict access to features when needed to protect the community. Archive review and moderation decisions remain associated with the server that received the submission, even when approved entries are visible globally.

Report problems through the server's moderation process. For private privacy requests, contact the operator through the Support Server linked in the bot's help command; public feedback channels are not confidential.
