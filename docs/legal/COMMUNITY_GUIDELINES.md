# Lemegeton Community Guidelines

**Effective date: November 24, 2026**

These guidelines apply to community features and submissions made through Lemegeton.

## Respect other members

Do not use submissions, discussions, reports, or other bot features to harass, threaten, impersonate, dox, or target people. Do not spam submissions, manipulate votes, abuse reports, or attempt to disrupt a server.

## Copyrighted uploads and attribution

Lemegeton allows community uploads of copyrighted material, including manga panels, comic pages, screenshots, illustrations, excerpts, and fan edits, when shared as part of relevant discussion, commentary, reviews, recommendations, collections, or community contributions. Copyrighted status alone is not a reason for automatic rejection.

Credit the original creator and link to a legitimate source where reasonably available. Attribution is not a substitute for permission or a legal exception. Users remain responsible for their individual uploads; Lemegeton does not grant rights in third-party works or determine that every upload is lawful. Do not falsely claim ownership or evade a valid rights-holder removal.

Quotes are limited by the bot to 280 characters.

For rights-holder reports and formal notices, use the [DMCA & Copyright Policy](DMCA.md) and [Copyright & Content Complaints Procedure](COPYRIGHT_AND_CONTENT_COMPLAINTS.md). Do not post private legal or personal information in public channels.

## Mark spoilers

Use the stated chapter, volume, episode, season, or other spoiler cutoff in discussions. Keep replies within that boundary and mark later material using Discord's spoiler formatting. Give submissions accurate spoiler labels and avoid misleading other members.

## Keep submissions appropriate

Use age-restricted features only in channels Discord marks as NSFW and follow the rules of the server where you post. Do not submit illegal, exploitative, threatening, or otherwise prohibited material. External links must use HTTPS where the feature requires it and must not be used to mislead members.

## Moderation and reports

Moderators may reject, hide, or remove material and restrict access to features when needed to protect a community. Archive review and moderation decisions remain associated with the server that received the submission, even when approved entries are visible globally.

Report community problems through the server's moderation process or the bot's available report controls. Include the relevant message, entry, or submission reference and a concise explanation. Do not use public feedback channels for confidential privacy or legal requests.

Where a moderator action supports review, ask the server's moderators to review it in the originating server. Lemegeton does not promise a fixed response time or a universal appeal mechanism across independently operated Discord servers.
