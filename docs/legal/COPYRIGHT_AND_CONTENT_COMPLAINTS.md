# Copyright & Content Complaints Procedure

**Effective date: November 24, 2026**

Lemegeton permits community uploads of copyrighted media, including manga panels and other fan-submitted material, as described in the [DMCA & Copyright Policy](DMCA.md). This procedure explains how to report a specific submission believed to infringe copyright or another right. Copyrighted status alone does not establish infringement, and a report does not automatically establish that a use is unlawful.

## 1. Where to send a report

Contact the operator privately through the Support Server linked in the bot's `/help` command. Ask for the copyright/content report route. Do not publish personal details, legal correspondence, or evidence in a public Discord channel or GitHub issue.

The operator must keep that reporting route monitored and publish a direct contact method if the Support Server is unavailable.

## 2. Include enough information to locate and assess the material

A useful report should include:

- Your name and a reliable contact method, and whether you are the rights holder or authorised to act for them.
- Identification of the copyrighted work or other right claimed to be affected.
- The exact Lemegeton archive entry, Discord message, attachment, URL, or other location of the material.
- A short explanation of why you believe the material is unauthorised.
- Any supporting information that can be shared safely, such as the original work's public URL or proof of authority.
- A statement that the information in the report is accurate to the best of your knowledge.

Do not send identity documents or unrelated personal information unless the operator explains why they are necessary and provides a secure method.

## 3. Review and response

The operator should:

1. Acknowledge receipt where a safe contact method is available.
2. Preserve the report and relevant minimal evidence with access restricted to people handling the complaint.
3. Assess whether the identified material is within Lemegeton's control, whether the report is sufficiently specific, and whether a legal or platform process applies.
4. Where appropriate, restrict access to the specific material while the report is assessed.
5. Contact the submitter or relevant server moderators where necessary and lawful.
6. Record the decision, action taken, reason, date, and any review route.
7. Notify the reporter of the outcome where legally and operationally appropriate.

The operator may remove or restrict access to material that appears to violate these Guidelines, platform rules, or applicable law. A complaint does not automatically establish infringement, and no fixed response or removal deadline is promised by this procedure. Applicable statutory deadlines take priority.

## 4. Disputes and review

If material is restricted, the affected submitter may provide a concise response explaining their rights or permission to share it. The operator will consider that response where appropriate and lawful. This is an internal review process and does not replace any formal counter-notice, court process, platform appeal, or other right available under applicable law.

For formal U.S. DMCA notices and counter-notifications, follow the [DMCA & Copyright Policy](DMCA.md). The operator's Support Server route is not represented as a registered DMCA agent contact.

## 5. Scope and limitations

Lemegeton may store metadata, attribution, links, excerpts, manga panels, screenshots, illustrations, fan edits, and other user-submitted media in community archives. The platform permits these categories of community contribution, but it does not grant users rights in third-party works or guarantee that each particular upload is lawful. The operator can act only on content and services within its control; Discord, hosting providers, and external websites may have separate reporting procedures.

For privacy issues connected with a complaint, see the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). For ordinary community issues, see the [Community Guidelines](COMMUNITY_GUIDELINES.md).
