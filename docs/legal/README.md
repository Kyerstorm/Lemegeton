## Important

Lemegeton v3 is still under development. The internal documents identify deployment decisions and release gates; they do not certify that a hosted deployment is compliant or that every deletion, expiry, or notification workflow is automated.

The operator must complete deployment-specific contact details, controller/processor roles, lawful bases, provider entities, transfer arrangements, retention periods, and operational ownership before public production use.
